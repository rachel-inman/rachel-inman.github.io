Why did you major in philosophy?

Philosophy was personally significant to me in my teens and gave me tools to examine the evangelical worldview I grew up in.

My philosophical interests center around epistemology and ethics. In junior year, I focused on the epistemology of disagreement: the conditions under which it's sensible to change your mind when someone disagrees with you. In senior year, I wrote my thesis, advised by Peter Singer, on the ethics of distributed vs. individual responsibility, using climate change as a lens.
